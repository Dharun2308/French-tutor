# Phase 5 — focused sessions and progress

Status: implemented design, 2026-09-02.

## Ten-minute session

A 12-card plan approximates the approved mix using largest whole-card shares: 5 due (40%), 3 weakest (25%), 2 listening (20%), and 2 recent corrections (15%). Items are deduplicated, then empty buckets are backfilled from the strongest available personal evidence. Listening cards hide text; the other cards require French production.

## Analytics

The progress view reports evidence, not points: production/listening accuracy, reviewed and mastered items, recurring weaknesses reduced, and recent real-tutor usage. “Mastered” means review-state evidence with at least three attempts, at least 80% success, stability of at least 21 days, and no recent failure. “Weak reduced” compares a current weak score below the action threshold with prior failure evidence.

TCF/NCLC is deliberately a readiness panel, not an estimated score. It shows which skills have evidence and says that only an official or realistic timed mock can establish a level.

## Weekly review

The weekly page always shows deterministic database facts. An explicit button may ask the configured structured-AI provider for a short summary, cached by local week. It never runs automatically and never invents counts.
